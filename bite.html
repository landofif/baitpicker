Source: FishOnMC wiki, Fishing Stats. Bite speed is only the wait from the bobber hitting the water to the fish particles. The time from the particles to the actual bite comes on top of this and is not in the wiki formula. Max wait = 20 s - 0.02 s per point, floor 3 s (reached at 850). Min wait = a quarter of the max, 5 s - 0.005 s per point, floor 1.5 s (reached at 700). The boosts are added to your pole value; that they stack by addition is assumed, the wiki does not say. The average assumes the wait is picked evenly between min and max, which is also not stated.
