Source: FishOnMC wiki, Fishing Stats. Casts per hour = 3600 / (average wait until the particles appear + the seconds after the particles you typed). The wiki's bite speed only covers the wait until the particles. The time from the particles to the actual bite, the minigame length (line strength has no formula) and the recast are not in any formula we have, so that number is yours to set. The average wait assumes the wait is picked evenly between min and max, and the boosts are assumed to add up; neither is stated on the wiki. Rarity and size are rolled once per cast, shards and pets are separate chances per cast. Bait is not counted; it only raises the odds above this (see the bait picker). Fish frenzy adds +100 bite speed, +250 luck, +250 scale, +50 prospect and +25% pet luck (from the server's chat lines).
