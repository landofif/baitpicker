Source: FishOnMC wiki, Fishing Stats. Raw: common 55 - 0.02 x luck, rare 30 + 0.002 x luck, epic 10 + 0.004 x luck, legendary 4 + 0.006 x luck, mythical 1 + 0.008 x luck, in percent. A rarity that would go below 0 cannot be caught; the odds shown are rescaled so the rest add up to 100%, as the bait picker does. The raw formulas always add up to 100 by themselves.
